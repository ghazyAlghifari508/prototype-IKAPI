## 1. Confirmed Product Requirements

The following decisions have been explicitly confirmed by the product owner.

### 1.1 Product identity

IKAPI Store is a digital bookstore/marketplace intended to serve the IKAPI Jawa Barat ecosystem.

Its main user-facing purpose is to help people discover, select, purchase, and read books.

Do not reposition this product as a SaaS application, technology showcase, payment service, or administrative platform.

### 1.2 Catalog data

The current book catalog uses **simulation data**.

The seven Standard Ebooks titles are demonstration assets, not verified IKAPI inventory.

Their listed prices are simulation values, not official publisher prices.

Preserve the demo data for prototype purposes, but never present it as verified official inventory, real sales data, or actual IKAPI publisher offerings.

### 1.3 E-book access

Purchased e-books must be read through the in-browser reader.

Users must NOT be given an e-book download action.

This is a product requirement intended to reduce unauthorized redistribution. Do not imply that the reader can eliminate piracy completely.

### 1.4 Payments

Midtrans is part of the intended purchasing process.

Payment interactions belong primarily in checkout and order-related screens, not in an oversized promotional homepage section.

### 1.5 Events and community

Events and community features are genuine application requirements.

Do not delete them from the product.

However, a required feature does not automatically need a large homepage section. Its placement must correspond to user needs.

## 2. Critical Problem: The Homepage Has Become a Feature Showcase

The current prototype contains content such as:

- A large e-reader demonstration section.
- A large Midtrans checkout demonstration section.
- A duplicate catalog section titled "Tiga judul lain di katalog yang sama."
- Technical explanations about payment processing.
- Prototype disclaimers written directly into consumer-facing marketing copy.
- Generic paragraphs added merely to fill space.

These sections create an unfocused experience.

A bookstore visitor should primarily be able to:

1. Understand that this is a place to browse books.
2. Search for a title, author, or publisher.
3. Explore book categories.
4. Discover available books.
5. View a book's details.
6. Continue toward purchase.
7. Find relevant events and community information if interested.

Evaluate EVERY homepage section against these needs.

If a section does not support them, do not preserve it merely because the old prototype contains it.

**Important correction to previous instructions:**

Earlier design prompts requested preservation of existing sections and visual demonstrations of the e-reader and Midtrans.

That instruction resulted in features being overrepresented on the homepage.

It is now superseded.

Preserve the functionality and product requirements, NOT the unnecessary homepage sections.

## 5. Rewrite All User-Facing Copy

Apply an explicit UX-writing quality standard across the affected homepage.

### Required principles

1. Write in natural Indonesian.
2. Speak to bookstore customers, not software engineers.
3. Prefer familiar words over technical jargon.
4. Use short, direct, meaningful phrases.
5. Every heading must accurately describe the content beneath it.
6. Every description must help the user understand a feature or make a decision.
7. Avoid explanations that repeat information already visible.
8. Do not use unnatural corporate phrasing.
9. Avoid forced Gen-Z slang.
10. Do not invent claims to make copy more persuasive.
11. Do not automatically generate a description under every heading.
12. Delete unnecessary text instead of constantly rewriting it.

Do not assume that a section needs a subtitle.

### Copy audit and required decisions

**A. "Baca di browser, tanpa unduh berkas."**

Action: REMOVE from homepage along with the reader showcase section.

Do not merely rephrase it.

Reader-related instructions should be placed where the user actually accesses books.

**B. "Pembayaran lewat Midtrans Snap."**

Action: REMOVE from homepage with the payment showcase.

In checkout, use a natural action-oriented heading such as:

"Pilih metode pembayaran"

Only display the payment methods supported by the actual integration.

**C. "Contoh agenda untuk pratinjau prototipe. Jadwal resmi diumumkan IKAPI Jawa Barat."**

Action: REMOVE from consumer-facing homepage copy.

Prototype status belongs in development documentation or controlled presentation context, not arbitrary marketing descriptions.

Do not replace this with a fabricated claim about upcoming official activities.

**D. "Tiga judul lain di katalog yang sama."**

Action: REMOVE the duplicate section entirely.

**E. "Marketplace buku digital resmi penerbit anggota IKAPI Jawa Barat. Dikelola untuk distribusi buku daerah yang legal dan terukur."**

Action: REWRITE the footer copy.

Suggested direction:

"Jelajahi buku digital dari penerbit anggota IKAPI Jawa Barat."

Do not claim that the seven demo books are real IKAPI inventory.

Use the copy as a description of the intended platform, not verification of existing listings.

**F. "PDF, bisa diunduh dari riwayat pesanan."**

Action: CLARIFY the object.

E-books must NOT be downloadable.

If the project specification confirms downloadable invoices, use:

"Unduh invoice"

Only in the appropriate order or invoice screen.

Do not imply the e-book itself can be downloaded.

**G. "Pilihan penerbit bulan ini"**

Action: FIX the semantic mismatch.

The section currently displays book titles, not featured publishers.

Suggested heading:

"Pilihan Buku"

Do not claim that books were selected by editors or publishers unless such curation actually exists.

**H. "Katalog contoh dari domain publik, dipakai sebagai pratinjau data prototype."**

Action: REMOVE from consumer-facing section descriptions.

Keep simulation metadata in the prototype's fixture data and documentation.

**I. "Ribuan judul dari penerbit anggota IKAPI"**

Action: DO NOT use this as a claim about current inventory without evidence.

The current dataset contains seven demo books.

Use wording that does not assert unverified scale or inventory.

**J. Category counts such as "1 judul" and "2 judul"**

These counts refer to the demo dataset.

Remove them from homepage category navigation if they might be mistaken for the entire IKAPI catalog.

Do not replace them with invented category counts.

## 6. Content Hierarchy and CTA Quality

Use consistent action labels.

Examples:

- "Jelajahi Katalog"
- "Lihat Semua Buku"
- "Lihat Detail"
- "Tambah ke Keranjang"
- "Pilih Metode Pembayaran"
- "Baca Buku"
- "Lihat Agenda"

These are examples, not instructions to add all actions to the homepage.

Every CTA must correspond to a meaningful destination or prototype interaction.

Avoid generic actions such as:

- "Pelajari Lebih Lanjut" without a clear object.
- "Temukan Keajaiban" or similar marketing clichés.
- "Baca Sekarang" when the user does not yet have access.
- "Beli Sekarang" if the interaction does not actually lead toward buying.

Do not show an enabled action that leads nowhere without explicitly documenting the prototype limitation.

## 8. Content Accuracy Rules

Do not present simulation data as real commercial data.

Explicitly distinguish in project data/documentation:

- Demo catalog records.
- Simulated prices.
- Unverified event information.
- Intended product features.
- Implemented functionality.
- Prototype-only interactions.

Avoid turning these distinctions into repetitive visible badges or disclaimers.

If the prototype will be shown as a realistic product demo, preserve the simulation status in its project context rather than pretending to have official inventory.

Do not fabricate:

- Publisher affiliations for Standard Ebooks titles.
- Real customer reviews.
- Sales rankings.
- Official IKAPI book quantities.
- Payment success confirmations.
- Upcoming event dates.
- Real transaction invoices.
